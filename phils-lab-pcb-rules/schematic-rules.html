A. Tools & Part Selection

S1📺Choose the MCU from the vendor’s product catalog by what the project needs: peripherals, max core clock, flash/RAM size, price.
S2📺The datasheet drives everything — download it from the manufacturer’s page and keep it open through the whole design.
S3📺Don’t copy tutorials one-to-one. Apply the method to your own design.

B. Project Setup & Title Block

S4📺✅File → New Project with a sensible name; keep the same name across schematic, PCB and outputs.
S5📺✅Fill in the title block before anything else: title, issue date, revision (Rev A, B…), company/name.
S6✅A simple design fits a single A4 sheet; hierarchical sheets only when complexity demands.

C. Symbols & Footprints — Library Discipline

S7📺Create your own schematic symbols and footprints. Never download from random internet sources.
S8📺Libraries can be project-local or global — choose deliberately.
S9📺Symbol name = full manufacturer part number. Reference designators: U for ICs, D for diodes, X for crystals.
S10📺For high-pin-count parts use the pin table to enter all pins quickly.
S11📺Pin names/numbers come from the datasheet — read the column of your exact package.
S12📺Check pin-attributes: standard vs high drive, 5V-tolerant, open-drain, IO structure.
S13📺Symbol arrangement: positive power pointing up, GND down, IO on left/right.
S14📺Grid discipline: 100 mil grid for symbols and schematics — never deviate.
S15📺Verify every footprint against the datasheet mechanical section.
S16📺✅Minimum fields per symbol: datasheet link, description, manufacturer, MPN, Mouser, DigiKey, LCSC.

D. Schematic Organization & Net Labeling

S17📺✅Organize in functional blocks with bold text titles: USB-C, MCU, SWD, etc.
S18📺Label every single net. Short, concise names instead of auto-generated Net-(U1-PA21).
S19📺✅Structured naming PERIPHERAL.SIGNAL (UART0.TX, I2C0.SDA). Differential pairs must end in −/+ (USB.D−, USB.D+).
S20📺Value notation: +3V3 not 3.3V, 5k1 not 5.1k, 10u not 10uF, 0R not 0.
S21📺GND symbols always point down.
S22📺✅One named rail per conversion stage (+VUSB → +5V → +3V3).
S23📺For jumps across the sheet use global labels (Ctrl+L).
S24📺✅No-connect flag (Q) on every unused pin.
S25📺Crossed connections (TX→RX, RTS→CTS) are a classic mistake — triple-check them.

E. USB-C Input

S26📺Use a generic 16-pin USB 2.0 type-C receptacle symbol.
S27📺✅VBUS ranges 4.5–5.5V → use +VUSB power flag rather than “+5V” at the connector.
S28📺✅CC1 and CC2 each need their own 5.1 kΩ pull-down. Advertises USB-C sink.
S29📺✅Tie D+/D+ and D−/D− pairs together directly for USB FS.
S30📺Every connection to the external world gets ESD protection (USBLC6-2SC6).
S31📺✅Tie the connector SHIELD to GND.
S32📺✅Placeholder Pi filter on VBUS: C — 0R — C.

F. 3V3 Regulation

S33📺LDO = simple/cheap; switcher = efficient but more parts.
S34📺✅SPX3819 (SOT-23-5): up to 500mA, low noise. Check datasheet capacitance requirements.
S35📺✅EN pin tied straight to the input rail.
S36📺BP pin: 10–100 nF lowers output voltage noise.

G. Decoupling & BOM Consolidation

S37📺✅VCORE needs its own external 470nF. Never omit datasheet-mandated caps.
S38📺Do NOT parallel a 10µF and a 0.1µF in similar packages — anti-resonances in the PDN.
S39📺✅One decoupling capacitor per power pin.
S40📺BOM consolidation: reuse the same handful of values everywhere (470n, 5k1, 0R).
S41📺10µF in 0805, not smaller. Voltage rating ≥2× the rail. Dielectric X5R/X7R.
S42📺✅Prefer JLCPCB “basic” parts for passives.

H. MCU Support Circuitry

S43📺✅NRST: pull-up to VDD required. Never a 0R.
S44📺✅ROSC: 100kΩ but must be 0.1% tolerance, ≤25 ppm/°C.
S45📺✅VREF− goes to GND through a 0R link so it can be opened later.

I. Crystal

S46📺Internal oscillator fine for casual use; crystal needed for UART/USB/precise timing.
S47📺✅Package: Crystal_GND24 (3225). Pins 2/4 are case shield → tie to GND.
S48📺C_ext = 2 × (CL − C_stray), C_stray ≈ 3–5 pF. Reference: ST AN2867.
S49📺Check crystal tolerance and frequency-stability ppm.
S50📺Series resistor on drive pin only if MCU overdrives crystal.
S51📺Some MCUs need external 1 MΩ parallel resistor — verify in datasheet.

J. USB-UART Bridge

S52📺CH340E: ~$0.4–0.65, MSOP-10, dead simple alternative to FTDI.
S53📺✅CH340E V3 pin: VCC=3.3V → tie V3 to VCC; VCC=5V → V3 gets only a decoupling cap.
S54📺✅TX↔RX and RTS↔CTS cross between bridge and MCU.

K. Pin Planning

S55📺Use vendor graphical pinmux (TI SysConfig) for legal pin choices.
S56📺Re-assign MCU pins whenever it makes the PCB easier, even mid-layout.

L. Adding a Peripheral

S57📺Before committing: check stock at LCSC/JLCPCB, supply ranges, and assembly class.
S58📺Find from the datasheet which pin actually needs the decoupling cap.
S59📺I2C pull-ups to logic rail. Check if interrupt outputs are push-pull or open-drain.

M. Annotation, Net Classes, ERC

S60📺Re-annotate in reading order: left→right, top→bottom.
S61📺✅Define net classes with colors in the schematic, assign by wildcard patterns (/USB*).
S62📺Run ERC and finish at zero errors.
S63📺Re-open footprint assignment as a final pass.

N. Mounting Holes & Fiducials

S64📺Add mounting-hole symbols tied to GND. M2 or M3; auto-excluded from BOM.
S65📺Add fiducials: 1 mm copper dot, 2 mm mask opening. Minimum 3 per assembled side.
S66📺Tag-Connect TC2030: footprint-only, zero BOM cost. Connect VCC, GND, SWDIO, SWCLK, NRST.
S67📺Triple-check the schematic before moving to layout.
