A. Board Setup BEFORE Any Placement or Routing

P1📺Re-check the schematic at the start of PCB work.
P2📺Board Setup → Layers: disable everything unused. Keep copper, paste, silkscreen, mask, edge cuts.
P3📺✅Stackup: 2 layers, 1.6 mm nominal. Finish: lead-free HASL is cheapest; ENIG for very fine pitch or longer shelf life.
P4📺Solder mask settings from the manufacturer's capability page: mask expansion 0.1mm, min mask web 0.1mm.
P5📺✅Enter design-rule constraints before doing anything else.

P6📺✅Predefined sizes: signal 0.3mm; power 0.5/1.0mm; fine-pitch 0.15/0.25mm. One via for the whole board: 0.7/0.3mm.
P7📺✅Differential-pair presets: width 0.3/gap 0.3; relaxed to 0.25/0.25 to enter small USB pads.
P8📺Netclass values must agree with constraints.
P9📺Board Setup → Net Classes → "Import colors from schematic".

B. Layout Philosophy

P10📺Layout is the substantial part of PCB design. Place everything before routing anything.
P11📺Real products start from mechanical constraints — honor those first.
P12📺Section-based placement: every schematic block has a centerpiece. Place it first, build its section around it.
P13📺Order of criticality: decoupling/bypass caps and crystal first; reset RC and pull-ups last.
P14📺Grid: rough placement on 0.5–1mm, fine placement on 0.25mm. Hover the component-center cross before pressing M.
P15📺Read the ratsnest while placing.
P16📺Don't scrunch components. Leave room for fan-out, vias, rework, probes.
P17📺Re-pin the MCU during layout when it removes crossings/vias; fix the schematic, press F8 to re-import.
P18📺✅All components on one side unless there's a reason not to — single-side assembly is much cheaper.

C. Outline, Origins, Mechanical

P19📺Prefer round-number dimensions and round center-to-center mounting hole spacings.
P20📺Outline on Edge.Cuts: lines (Ctrl+Shift+L) + 90° arcs for rounded corners.
P21📺Keep ~0.5mm clearance from board edge for copper, components, and silk.
P22📺Grid origin AND drill/place-file origin at the bottom-left corner of the board (IPC).
P23📺✅Spread the 3 fiducials as far apart as possible — deliberately asymmetric.
P24📺Courtyards must stay on the board and must not overlap (DRC error).

D. Placement Details

P25📺Decoupling caps: as close as feasible, courtyard-clear. Think about the whole current loop including ground return.
P26📺The schematic carries design intent: C6 drawn at pin 7 means C6 is placed at pin 7.
P27📺Crystal: close to its pins, away from aggressive signals (USB, fast edges). Function over aesthetics.
P28📺Multiple pads on one net are not equivalent: find the right pad by cross-referencing schematic + datasheet.
P29📺✅ESD protection between connector and circuit, close to the connector.
P30📺Routing under connector shells: solder mask is NOT a reliable insulator.
P31📺Final silkscreen cleanup happens at the very end.
P32📺Use the 3D viewer (Alt+3) constantly. “Never use a part without a 3D model.”

E. Zones & Via Pre-placement

P33📺✅2-layer strategy: top = components + signal + power; bottom = one solid GND plane. Repour with B.
P34📺✅Mounting-hole pads: change connection to solid — nothing is soldered there.
P35📺Pre-place vias BEFORE routing. Rule: ≥1 GND via adjacent to every GND pad. Never in-pad.
P36📺A via under a component’s belly is sometimes acceptable — some sensors forbid copper underneath; check the datasheet.

F. Routing — General Technique

P37📺Route in order of criticality: decoupling links → crystal + ROSC → SWD → NRST → signals → USB diff pair → CC → power → GND.
P38📺Stick to ONE standard signal width (0.3mm) unless impedance or current say otherwise.
P39📺Neck-down only through tight gaps and widen again. W/Shift+W cycle preset widths.
P40📺Pad entries straight-on at 90°. No acute angles — acid traps are a real fab issue.
P41📺45° bends. Spacing is the #1 cure for crosstalk.
P42📺Keep traces as short as reasonable — long traces are unintentional antennas.
P43📺Routing under the MCU body is fine when clean.
P44📺After routing, drag-tune segments (D): straighten, give clearance, keep clean entries.

G. Routing — Specific Nets

P45📺Decoupling caps first: shortest and widest possible links.
P46📺Crystal: short, symmetric. Put a guard ring of GND stitching vias around the crystal.
P47📺VREF/reference returns: route wide (0.5+).
P48📺✅USB differential pair: press 6 (Route → Differential Pair). No length matching needed at FS. Avoid vias on the pair.
P49📺CC1/CC2: routed to symmetric pull-downs.

H. Power Routing

P50📺Route power along the power-flow path: connector → Pi filter → LDO input cap → LDO → output cap → distribution.
P51📺Golden rule: trace → capacitor pad → device power pin. Never trace → pin with cap hanging to the side.
P52📺Power widths: match the pad (0.6/0.8mm).
P53📺Layer changes: press V while routing. Never under critical signals. Repour (B) after every change.

I. Top Pour, Stitching, Island Hygiene

P54📺✅Extend GND zone to both layers, repour.
P55📺Nudge pre-placed vias out of thermal-relief zones.
P56📺Pads the pour can’t reach: finish with short manual GND tracks.
P57📺Thermal relief vs solid: reliefs for THT; solid where thermal mass is small.
P58📺Island & antenna hygiene: lone via feeding a tiny copper islet = high-frequency antenna → delete or join properly.
P59📺✅Via stitching: tie top and bottom GND pours together everywhere. “Vias are essentially free.”

J. Teardrops

P60📺✅Teardrops: widen the trace into the pad. Video skips them; published files enable them on PTH + SMD pads + vias.

K. Silkscreen & Board Identification

P61📺No bottom silkscreen if nothing needs it.
P62📺✅Put on the silk: board title/name, revision, designer initials, date code, company/logo.
P63📺Keep silk off holes and exposed copper (silk-on-pad worsens solderability).

L. DRC

P64📺Run DRC repeatedly while designing.
P65📺Triage report: solder-mask bridges on fine-pitch → set to ignore; clearance mismatches → align the numbers.
P66📺Awkward footprint clearance: edit the footprint instance — save into the board only.
P67📺✅Finish at 0 errors AND 0 warnings — every exclusion deliberate and commented.
P68📺Triple-check schematic, pinout and routing before generating manufacturing files.

M. Manufacturing Outputs

P69📺Three deliverables: Gerbers (fab), BOM + CPL/pick-and-place (assembly).
P70📺✅Gerbers → into mfr/: F.Cu, B.Cu, F.Paste, F.Silkscreen, F.Mask, B.Mask, Edge.Cuts. Format: extended X2, use drill/place file origin.
P71📺✅Drill files: Excellon, PTH and NPTH as separate files, millimeters, decimal format.
P72📺✅Zip Gerbers + drills into one archive.
P73📺✅CPL header for JLCPCB: Designator, Value, Footprint, Mid X, Mid Y, Rotation, Layer.
P74📺✅BOM: export from Schematic → Edit Symbol Fields. Check line-by-line — human error is real.

N. Ordering at JLCPCB

P75📺Upload Gerber zip → confirm auto-detected layer count and dimensions → inspect preview AND Gerber viewer.
P76📺Typical: 5 pcs, FR4, 1.6mm, green mask + white silk, lead-free HASL.
P77📺“Confirm production file” (+~$1) — always.
P78📺Assembly: top side only. Enable “confirm parts placement.”
P79📺Upload BOM + CPL → go through row by row.
P80📺Expect to fix rotations — every ECAD tool and assembler uses a different 0° convention. Spacebar for 90° steps.
P81📺Declare usage (research/education, DIY) for customs. Save to cart, ship.
