Troubleshooting: every problem in one place

Every lesson has a “When it doesn’t work” table. Here they all are (212 problems), grouped by the kind of symptom. Find what you are seeing, read the likely cause and the fix, and follow the lesson link for the full explanation.

Coach’s method: don’t guess, test

Troubleshooting has three steps: look closely at the symptom → list the likely causes → rule each one in or out with a simple test. Before anything else, check these three: is the USB cable a data cable? What does the Serial Monitor print at the right speed? Does the supply hold up under load? The full method, with a flowchart, is in the troubleshooting handbook in lesson 6.4, and the ten most common errors are on the cheat sheet.
