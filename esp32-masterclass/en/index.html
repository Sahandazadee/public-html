How does this course work?

🧒 From absolute zero

No prerequisites. We even explain voltage, current and resistance from scratch, using water in a pipe as the example.

🔍 No code without an explanation

Every complete program in the course has been tested with the real ESP32 compiler (core 3.3.12). Under each code block there is a piece-by-piece walkthrough: what each part does and why it is done that way. Click any explanation to highlight its lines in the code.

🔌 Standard wiring diagrams

Red for power, black for ground, colors for signals. Each diagram comes with a wire-by-wire connection table.

🟢 No hardware to buy

69 circuits come with a ready-made Wokwi file, so you can simulate them free in your browser. (Bluetooth, ESP-NOW, OTA updates and the camera can't be simulated in Wokwi.)

💬 Pop-up glossary

Hover over (or tap) dotted words such as PWM or GPIO to see a plain-English meaning.

🎯 A strict coach

Every lesson has quizzes, exercises and a "coach's note". Only tick "I finished this lesson" once you have answered every quiz correctly.

A word from your coach before you start

Go through this course in order. Each lesson builds on the one before it. If you rush straight to the ESP32-CAM, you'll get stuck at the first error, because you won't know whether the problem is the power supply, the pin or the code. One lesson a day, with the exercises done, beats ten lessons in one night.

What should I buy to get started?

You can get started with nothing but the Wokwi simulator. To build for real, this basic kit covers most of the course. The exact shopping list, with quantities, buying stage and the lesson each part is for, is in the toolbox, and tips for choosing a board are in lesson 1.2. Once your parts arrive, do lesson 1.4 (hands-on bench) first.
