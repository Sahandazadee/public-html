Whenever you want to connect a part, come here first. Click any pin to see what it can do and what can go wrong. Use the buttons above the map to highlight a family of pins (for example all the ADC pins or all the touch pins). Each group is explained in full in lesson 1.3.

Official Espressif pinout

Does your board have 30 pins?

The popular "ESP32 DOIT DevKit V1" has only 30 pins: it doesn't bring out the flash pins (6 to 11), GPIO0 (reachable only through the BOOT button) or one of the GND pins, and its pins are in a different order from this map. But the GPIO numbers are the same, so always go by the GPIO number, not by the physical position. On some boards "D2" is printed, which simply means GPIO2.
